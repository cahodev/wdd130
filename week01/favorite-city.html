Santiago, Chile

Santiago is the vibrant capital city of Chile, nestled in a valley surrounded by the majestic Andes Mountains. It offers a unique blend of historic architecture, modern skyscrapers, and beautiful urban parks where you can enjoy stunning views of the snow-capped peaks in the background.

One of the best features of the city is its culture and location. You can visit historic landmarks like Plaza de Armas or San Cristobal Hill during the day, and easily travel to either world-class ski resorts or the Pacific coast withi just a couple of hours.
